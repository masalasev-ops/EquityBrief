Item 1. Description of Registrant’s Securities to be Registered.

The description of the general terms and provisions of the 11% Callable STock Return Income DEbt SecuritiesSM due April 28, 2009 issued by Merrill Lynch & Co., Inc. (the “Callable STRIDES”), payable on the maturity date with Cisco Systems, Inc. common stock, set forth in the Preliminary Term Sheet, dated March 26, 2008, the Product Supplement STRIDES-2, dated November 9, 2007, and the MTN Prospectus Supplement, General Prospectus Supplement and Prospectus, each dated March 31, 2006, attached hereto as Exhibit 99(A), is hereby incorporated by reference and contains certain proposed terms and provisions. The description of the Callable STRIDES contained in the Term Sheet to be filed pursuant to Rule 424(b) under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, under Registration Statement Number 333-132911, which will contain the final terms and provisions of the Callable STRIDES, including the maturity date of the Callable STRIDES, is hereby deemed to be incorporated by reference into this Registration Statement and to be a part hereof.
