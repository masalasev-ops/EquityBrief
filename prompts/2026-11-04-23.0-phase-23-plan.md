## The phase in plain words

**What it does.** It gives every rule about 20 years and every kind of market to be judged on, 2008 included, instead of the 7 years it has today, and it judges a family over all 1,500 stocks together.

**What goes wrong today.** A verdict over 2022 to 2026 needs years to reach 300 trades, and every sweep scores from 2019-01-02 (`SweepColumns.FirstScored`), so the test is reading years the setting has already seen. Worse, a trade whose stock's prices stop returns no result at all today (`FamilyWalks`, `SweepWalk`), so the stocks that failed are the ones that vanish.

**What changes.** A store of older holdings, prices and filing figures, kept apart from the bars and read by no night. A data check over it before anything is tested. A pooled verdict beside each index's own. New searches of the starting settings, which come to you as proposals. At the end one restart, with your word.

**Example.** The S&P 500 dip has 284 test trades, the S&P 400's 324 and the S&P 600's 206. Alone, two of them miss the 300 minimum. Pooled they count 814 and get a verdict. And 2008 to 2018 are years no live setting has ever seen, so those years are the first fair test of them.
