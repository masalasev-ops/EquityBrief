## Why it joins the restart

A variant is an evaluator in `CandidateEvaluators` and the fill that buys it in `TradeFills`, both among the sources every
live rule's version pins, as 22.44 found for the entry check's wait entries. Registering the skip as a variant moves those
pins, so every live rule's version moves; it cannot stand beside the live rules as a variant until the restart.

## The rule as measured

- The typical daily move is Wilder's 14-session average true range from the stored adjusted bars, high to low and not
  close to close: each session's true range runs from the higher of its high and the close before to the lower of its
  low and the close before, the first fourteen averaged and each later one carried forward by Wilder's smoothing, read on
  the setup's session:
  the ledger's `risk_moves` is the buy less the stop over it, the buy being the session's close the plan is set at.
- Skipped: `risk_moves` under 1. GWW on the night of 2026-11-05, buy $1,451.39, stop $1,449.98, is the operator's case.
- The breakout's and the dip's stops never stand under one typical move in the stored years on any index, so the skip
  reaches the pullback and the drift alone.
